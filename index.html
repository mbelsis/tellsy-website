Everything you need, nothing you don't

Built for the moments when your hands are full and your head is fuller.

Speak naturally

“Next Friday at 5”, “in two hours”, “every Monday”, “from half past two to four”. Tellsy understands how people actually talk about time.

Four languages

English, Greek, French and Italian. Pick your everyday language and switch to another with one tap before you speak.

Asks when it matters

Said “at 8”? Tellsy asks morning or evening. No date heard? It asks when. Check each item on a card, or turn review off and go hands-free.

Your apps, or just Tellsy

Send each kind of item to Apple Calendar, Reminders or Notes, or keep it inside Tellsy only. You choose, per kind.

My items

Your week as a timeline. Tick off reminders, tick lines off a shopping list, edit notes, pin the ones that matter, add more by voice.

Reminders that ring

Reminders and meetings kept in Tellsy alert you on time, with Done and In 1 hour right on the notification.

Change it by voice

“Move the dentist to Monday at six”, “actually, better at eight”, “rename it to lunch with Anna”. No hunting for the item.

Wherever you are

From the Lock Screen, Control Center, the Action button, Siri, the Apple Watch, and a Today widget for what is next.

Private by design

No account, no server, no tracking. What you say is understood on your iPhone, and History can be locked with Face ID.
