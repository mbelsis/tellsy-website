Support

How can we help?

Tellsy has a full guide built in: open the app and tap the question mark.

Get in touch. Email support@tellsy.app. Say which iPhone and iOS version you have, and what you said or tapped. Please do not include private dictations unless you want to share them.

Common questions

A reminder did not ring

Tellsy rings for reminders kept in Tellsy (Settings › Where things go › Reminders › Tellsy only). Reminders sent to Apple Reminders are announced by Apple Reminders. Also check iOS Settings › Notifications › Tellsy › Allow Notifications.

The notification only says “Reminder”

iOS hides the words on a locked iPhone until Face ID recognises you; that is iOS's Show Previews setting, “When Unlocked” by default. To see them on the locked screen too, choose “Always” under iOS Settings › Notifications › Tellsy › Show Previews.

It wrote what I said in the wrong language

Tellsy listens in one language at a time. Tap the language you are about to speak on the main screen before recording, or change your default under Settings › Dictation language.

Recording stops too soon, or waits too long

Change the pause under Settings › Stopping. Notes and lists have their own, longer pause, because people stop to think. You can always tap the button to stop.

How do notes get into Apple Notes?

iOS gives apps no direct way to write into Apple Notes. Tellsy uses the share sheet (one tap on Notes), or a small Shortcut that saves without any taps. The setup guide in the app installs the Shortcut in two minutes.

Can I use Tellsy without Apple's apps?

Yes. Under Settings › Where things go, choose “Tellsy only” for each kind of item. Everything then lives in My items, where reminders ring and lists and notes can be edited. Those items exist only on your iPhone and in your backups: if you delete the app or lose your iPhone without a backup, they cannot be recovered.

Does Tellsy work with Siri and the Apple Watch?

Yes: “Hey Siri, voice command in Tellsy” in English, French and Italian (Siri does not support Greek), and a Watch app that sends what you say to your iPhone. The Siri phrases work on the iPhone; on the Watch, say “Hey Siri, open Tellsy”, then tap the button and the microphone. Away from your iPhone the Watch keeps what you dictate and says “Waiting for your iPhone”; it goes over when the iPhone is next in range, and the Watch then says “Saved on your iPhone.” Reviewing, saving and discarding a card need the iPhone within reach. On the Watch, tap the microphone on the keyboard screen and speak. The Watch writes in the language its keyboard is set to, so add every language you speak as a keyboard on your iPhone (Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards); it then appears on the Watch, where you switch language from the keyboard screen. Greek works with the in-app button, the Lock Screen button and the Action button.

The Apple Watch says to open Tellsy on my iPhone and allow access first

iOS asks for permission to use Reminders or Calendar only inside the app on the iPhone, never on the Watch. Nothing is lost: what you dictated is waiting in History. To clear it:

  1. Open Tellsy on the iPhone.
  2. Save a reminder there: the one you dictated is waiting in History, or dictate a new one and tap Save.
  3. iOS asks whether Tellsy may access your Reminders. Choose Allow Full Access. Tellsy needs full access because it also edits and completes reminders, not only adds them.
  4. From then on, saving works from the Watch as well.

The same happens once for Calendar, the first time you save a meeting. If you would rather not give access, choose “Tellsy only” under Settings › Where things go, and no permission is needed.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Tellsy is free to download and every feature is free for 10 days from the moment you tap Start free trial. After that, recording new items (by voice, by typing, with Siri, Shortcuts or the Apple Watch) needs Tellsy Pro, a one-time purchase with no subscription. Nothing you already have is locked or deleted: History and My items stay fully usable, you can still edit, tick off, share and delete, and reminders kept in Tellsy still ring. Settings shows how many days are left.

I bought Tellsy Pro. How do I restore it?

On a new or reinstalled iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account, Tellsy Pro normally comes back by itself. If it does not, open Settings in Tellsy and tap Restore Purchases; you may be asked to sign in to the App Store. Tellsy Pro is shared through Family Sharing, so a family member also taps Restore Purchases if it has not appeared. The free trial belongs to your Apple Account too, so reinstalling the app does not start it again. Payments, receipts and refunds are handled by Apple: see your purchase history in the App Store, or reportaproblem.apple.com for a refund.

History says a note was “Returned to Shortcut, not confirmed”. What does that mean?

A Shortcut you built took the note's text from Tellsy. Tellsy cannot see what the Shortcut did with it, so it does not call the note sent and never removes it automatically. Check that the note is in Notes, then tap Mark as saved; or tap Send to Notes to have Tellsy send it.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, History › Delete removes the items shown or everything; Settings has options to forget learned words. If History shows a notice that some entries could not be read, the copy Tellsy kept of them is deleted from that notice, or with Delete everything. Deleting the app removes all of its data from the iPhone. Items already sent to Calendar, Reminders or Notes are kept there; delete them in those apps.
